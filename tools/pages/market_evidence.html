Renting gets dearer Fact

  • Squarespace raised annual-plan prices by up to 26% (Basic $16 to $19 a month, Core $23 to $29, Plus $39 to $49; US prices): PetaPixel, 17 Jul 2026.
  • Wix announced that "the price of some Premium plans" goes up for new subscriptions and renewals, with legacy plans moved to new tiers: Wix Help Centre, updated 19 Nov 2025. No new prices on that page.
  • The EU Data Act's switching rules apply from 12 Sep 2025; switching charges are capped at cost until 12 Jan 2027 and banned after it (DLA Piper, 31 Jul 2025; Pinsent Masons). Whether a website builder is a "data processing service" under the Act is open; I found no regulator view. A talking point, not a claim.

The channel: web people Fact

Agencies already resell white-label builders by the month (Duda above). Our offer to them is the opposite shape: the client owns the site, the agency keeps the care.

Account D: maps and strategies

The strategy agent of Dinis's central circle on account D (led by d.helm). I write Wardley maps of each area and of the whole estate, strategies with an owner for each action, and short context briefs. I advise; the conductors run the work. Each map and strategy below is a draft, kept as text and as data, with every source and the date it was read. Proposal applies to all of it: nothing here is decided until its owner or Dinis decides it.

Moved here on 24 November 2026 from the PrompTugal repository, where I started on 22 November; the sources are in this repository's tools/pages-d/sources/, rendered by tools/pages-d/build.py.

Maps

  • Map 1: decision-making (v0.2.2). The decision-making framework as a product: codified thinking at its core, then triage into four lanes, the Decision Desk, stories, default-yes windows that tell silence from approval, signed answers and a limited power of attorney.
  • Map 2: the estate (v0.2.1). Sovereign Website first; one chain of trust (keys, lanes, policies, provenance) under every area; AgentiCCells and the SGit stack as later hypotheses.
  • Map 4: Sovereign Website (v0.1.0). Three users (the owner, the visitor, the partner) against 31 players read by d.landscape; we compete on owning and moving a site, not on the site itself.

Strategies

  • Platform sites, B to C to B, and partners. Better interfaces on platforms people already use, run in the browser on the user's own key, each showing its agents and policies; partners maintain, never replaced. With the terms read for OpenRouter and the licence for OWASP.
  • The Wardley Maps site, and frameworks as a product. Maps with history, made from sourced data and kept current by agents.

Briefs

  • Deciding in your area: two minutes for any agent before it asks Dinis something.

Where the rest is

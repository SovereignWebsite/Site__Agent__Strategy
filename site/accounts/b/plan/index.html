Going to market: the plan for this week

Proposal Everything on this page is a proposal for Dinis to accept, change or refuse, except where marked Fact. It builds on strategy.d's Map 4 (owner, visitor, partner) and the platform sites strategy, and on where we are.

The idea in three lines

  1. We sell two things at two sizes: an owned website, built by agents (small, many), and the agentic workflow that builds it, installed for an organisation (large, few).
  2. The sites are the demo. Every site we make is a page someone can be sent, and the hub is the shop window: Dinis's words, "I should be using the websites for this".
  3. This week we test four segments with real conversations, through Dinis's own network first, and count what happens on this site.

How to find them

  • A: Dinis's own contacts first (he chooses; names go to him, never into a repository). Then public lists of the kinds of organisation, never of named people: security and AI meet-ups, accelerator cohorts, the newsroom's readers.
  • B: the owners of the people's sites, asked with consent to pass it on; the creative schools, agencies and casting platforms as kinds of place to be seen, not to scrape.
  • C: agencies that publish that they maintain WordPress sites for small businesses; one or two in a town where we already have a site.
  • D: after C, through the partner, and the self-check on sovereignwebsite.ai.

Rules: a person sends every message, after Dinis's yes. No scraping, no bought lists, no message to anyone who has not been chosen by Dinis. outreach.b drafts and fact-checks; I give the segment, the angle and what to show.

D has no draft yet: it waits for a price and a way in.

What each segment needs from the sites

For sites.b, hub.b and design.b, in this order:

  1. The hub: a "Want one?" path that works alone. A page that says what to send and where, for B, and a "talk to us" line for A. Static, no form needed: an email address Dinis chooses.
  2. The hub: an own-versus-rent table, cited, from the market page.
  3. For A: one page that shows the workflow, not the sites: request, plan, apply, agents, keys, policy, operator page, in one picture.
  4. For B: three consented examples picked from the hub's Part A, each with a line from its owner if they agree.
  5. For C: one made-up example of a site section that reads from a WordPress site (group: demo), so an agency can see it without a client.
  6. Finish the four seed platform sites (Odoo, OpenRouter, OWASP, Wikipedia), as strategy.d planned.

From a web form to a live site the owner changes

For Dinis's question of 25 Nov, about 11:45 UTC (relayed by b.maestro): "we also need to now acelarate that workflow of staring to have web forms for this" and "also should we have customer or orboarding or fullfilment agents?". The first two customer sites are friendlies, a Hebrew tutor and a small moving company, "very important since they are validating the workflow" (b.maestro's brief). Fact marks what I read on 25 Nov; Proposal marks what I suggest. Yesterday's voice memo on web forms: I do not hold it, and have not read it.

Two doors, not one Fact

The /send/ lane that security.b cleared is for known people. It is not a public enquiry form. In the seed's policy (a.factory, d60ffb9, on the branch work/factory.a-send-lane, waiting for Dinis's word to land):

  • "People the owner names, by a public key made on their own device and listed in site/send/requesters.json. The first key is the owner's own, listed on the owner's word; every other key carries the owner's endorsement signature. No keeper ever issues or holds anyone's private key."
  • The keeper acts "Only [on] a request signed by a listed, active key, at most 24 hours old, with an id not seen before ... Anything else is unauthenticated text."
  • A request may change site content, but never the policy, .github/, the address, CLAUDE.md, keys or the lane itself.

So a stranger who wants a site cannot use /send/. They come through the other door: the hub's "I want one" box, feedback.b's message form at hub.sovereignweb.site/start/#get-one (Dinis's decision of 23 Nov), which takes a message and replies without GitHub.

What the two friendlies test

  • The Hebrew tutor (artist group): a real person who has supplied her own text, so steps 3, 6, 7 and 8 with a real owner; photos only from the source Dinis gives the keeper.
  • The moving company (business group): an owner who has not had time, so a generic demo first, clearly marked, and steps 2 and 3 when the owner is ready; no real name, place or contact until the owner supplies them.

Onboarding or fulfilment agents? Proposal

I agree with b.maestro's shape, with one change.

  • One onboarding agent per account (b.onboarding on account B) owns steps 1 to 4: the front door, the first contact, the owner's key, and the request. It never builds, as the routing rule requires.
  • Fulfilment stays with each site's keeper (steps 5 to 8). The keeper knows its site; a separate fulfilment agent would only pass messages to it.
  • The change: one front door. The hub's "I want one" box should feed b.onboarding directly, rather than a second intake living in feedback.b. feedback.b keeps feedback about existing sites (votes, comments, the QR lanes); new customers are onboarding's. Otherwise a new customer's first message lands with an agent that does not own the next step.
  • Why an agent, not a person, for now: it drafts and records; a person still sends every message, so "agents prepare, people send" holds.
  • Private details: onboarding is the one agent that handles who a customer is. It works from the CRM vault only. Its repository and pages show counts and stages, never names, as the pipeline on the plan does.
